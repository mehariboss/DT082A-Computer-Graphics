As part of the mini-projects, you are expected to:

- Prepare a set of mandatory-to-include slides for reporting your project progress.

- Submit your final presentation slides and your code in a .zip format.

## Overview of the final presentation session

During this session, all seven project teams will present their project outcomes and make a demo. Each project will be allocated 30 minutes, divided into:

- **Presentation and Demo (17–20 minutes):** Teams will give a concise presentation of their project and demonstrate their application. Demonstration of the developed tool is an integral part of this first section.
- **Questions, Answers, and Peer Discussion (20–23 minutes):** Students and instructors will ask questions, provide feedback, and engage in discussion.
  1. First, the reviewing team will comment and pose questions.
  2. Afterwards, each group is expected to pose questions regarding the project and the implementation.
  3. Finally, instructors will ask questions and provide feedback.

Each team should prepare:

- A short presentation summarizing their project goals, methodology, and expected outcomes.
- A demo showcasing the current state or concept of their project.
- A readiness for Q&A to clarify and refine ideas.

### Opponent role

The opponent prepares questions to be posed in the final project presentations. There is no need to prepare a report.

- Review the assigned group's slides and prepare three or more thoughtful questions to be asked after their presentation.
- Focus on their methodology, implementation, and presentation clarity.
- Provide constructive feedback during the presentation, ensuring comments are respectful and helpful (be kind and constructive).
- Additionally, each group is expected to engage in the discussion with at least one question.
